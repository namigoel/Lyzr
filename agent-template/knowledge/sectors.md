# Sector Reference

Valuation and momentum norms used as context when scoring a signal. These are desk heuristics, not live data — refreshed by the analyst periodically, not by the agent.

## Semiconductors
Cyclical, capex-sensitive. Momentum swings are large and often precede fundamentals by a quarter or two. Treat a single month of price action as weak evidence on its own.

## Fintech / Consumer Finance
Rate-sensitive. Watch for regulatory headline risk — a single enforcement action can move the name independent of fundamentals.

## Payments
Mature, moat-driven. Momentum is a weaker signal here than in growth sectors; valuation compression/expansion matters more.

## EV / Auto
High cash-burn risk for non-incumbents. A bear case that ignores balance-sheet runway is not a complete bear case for this sector.

## Gaming (reference only — mostly out of mandate at micro-cap)
High volatility, thin liquidity. Most names here fail the market-cap floor.
